Two-layer monetization: Discovery circles (₹2,999/session) feeding an annual inner circle (₹75,000/year).

Part 1 — Updated Business Plan

The Problem (Validated)

After 40+ operator interviews, the same sentence surfaced every time:

"I have no one honest to think with."

Senior operators are surrounded by people who need things from them — teams need certainty, investors need confidence, family needs stability. The actual thinking — the unresolved decisions, the doubts, the complexity — stays private. Existing solutions (therapy, networking, masterminds, coaching) each fail for specific structural reasons.

The gap: A peer-only, structured, no-stakes room for people carrying decision weight.

The Product

Layer 1 — Discovery Circle (₹2,999)
8 people. One Sunday. 3 hours. Application-screened. Structured 6-phase protocol. Functions as both the product and the vetting filter for the inner circle.

Layer 2 — Inner Circle (₹75,000/year)
Fixed cohort of 6–8. Monthly facilitated sessions. 12-month arc. Drawn exclusively from discovery circle alumni. This is the primary revenue engine.

The Protocol (Research-Backed)

Six phases across 3 hours:

  • Disruption (20 min) — Facilitator discloses first.
  • Context Rounds (40 min) — Escalating reciprocal questions.
  • Issue Presentation (50 min) — Live problem, experience-only responses.
  • Buffet Break (20 min) — Unstructured bonding.
  • Deep Closing (40 min) — Aron level 3 disclosures.
  • Nomination Ritual (10 min) — Each member nominates 2 for future circles.

Research basis: Aron et al. (1997) reciprocal disclosure, Yalom's group therapeutic factors, YPO Forum peer advisory methodology, Dunbar's intimacy layers.

"How do you think about GTM for a new product?"
Candor launch taught me: distribution must precede the date, not follow it. Warm channels outperform cold 10:1 for high-trust products. Credibility is borrowed before it's earned. Wrong audience kills right messaging.
